## Goal

Estimate the apparent pKa of methyl orange using absorbance at 531 nm.

## Steps

- Inspect the low-pH spectra for flattening or other unusual behavior.
- Fit a two-state equilibrium model at 531 nm.
- Report the apparent pKa and fitting uncertainty.
- Plot the fitted curve and residuals.
- Check how the result changes when individual pH conditions are excluded.
