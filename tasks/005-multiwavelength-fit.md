- **Status:** active
- **Created:** 2026-11-22
- **Updated:** 2026-11-22
- **Due:** 2026-12-09

## Goal

Compare a shared-pKa multiwavelength fit with the single-wavelength fit at 531 nm.

## Steps

- Review the spectra and crossing near 470 nm.
- Choose the wavelengths to include and record the reason in the judgment log.
- Fit a two-state model with a shared pKa across the selected wavelengths.
- Compare the pKa and fitting uncertainty with the single-wavelength result.
- Inspect residuals and check sensitivity to wavelength selection and excluded pH conditions.
